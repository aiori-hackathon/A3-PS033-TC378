# Original ambient score

The DiagDiscover product film uses **Quiet Discovery**, an original procedural
instrumental composed for this project on 14 October 2026. Its sounds were
synthesized from elementary oscillators, with a sparse mallet motif, warm
extended chords, a gentle pulse and stereo reflections. It contains no borrowed
reference-video audio, downloaded songs, sound-library samples or copied melody.

The final film mixes this score quietly beneath synthesized English narration,
with automatic music ducking during speech and a nine-second ending fade.
The final exported video was measured at -16.0 LUFS integrated loudness and
-3.4 dBFS true peak. Music and narration are included in the MP4; they require
no separate downloads or playback software beyond a normal video player.

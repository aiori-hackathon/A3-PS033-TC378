PUBLISHED / DISCOVERABLE

What DNS publishes.

Browse the signed service index in this application's diag.test zone. This reads DNS metadata only; it does not invoke a diagnostic service.

Scope: the managed local test zone. Browsing creates a temporary catalogue; the listed endpoints are not running services. This does not scan your LAN or discover every service on the Internet.

Connect to the local server to browse its published services.
Inspect service-index evidence

An accepted DNS index and service record do not establish TLS identity, access permission, availability or system health. Browsing uses a fresh local catalogue without starting its service endpoints. The next diagnostic run creates its own zone and running services.
